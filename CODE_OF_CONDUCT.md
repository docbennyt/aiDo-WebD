# Community conduct

Treat contributors with respect. Welcome questions and disagreement about ideas; avoid personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and publication of private information. Keep review focused on technical evidence and constructive feedback.

Report misconduct privately to the maintainer via the contact method on [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/docbennyt). Do not publish sensitive reports in public issues. Maintainers may remove content, issue warnings, restrict participation or block repeat offenders, proportionate to the conduct. Reports should be handled confidentially as far as reasonably possible. If a report concerns the maintainer, use GitHub's reporting channels. This applies to project spaces and representation of the project.
